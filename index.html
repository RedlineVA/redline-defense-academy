Frequently Asked Questions

Know before you train.

Do I need to own a firearm before taking a class?

Not necessarily. Contact Redline Defense Academy before registering so the instructor can confirm the equipment requirements for your specific course.

Are beginners welcome?

Yes. New shooters are welcome, and private instruction is available for students who prefer individualized coaching before joining a larger class.

Does a class guarantee a permit?

No. Training may satisfy or support a jurisdiction's education requirement, but permit approval is determined by the relevant government agency and the applicant's eligibility.

Can I book a private or small-group session?

Yes. Call or text to discuss private instruction, small-group training, or a class for your organization or group.

Where are classes held?

Locations vary by course. Contact the academy for the current class location, schedule, range details, and what to bring.

What should I bring?

Your exact equipment list depends on the course. After enrollment, you should receive course-specific instructions covering identification, firearm/ammunition needs, eye and ear protection, and other required materials.
